# Admin workspace

Mode: Operate. Audience: administrators and staff managing catalog, customers and recurring agreements. Scope: Shaheem-owned UI plus shared presentation, not backend or Alfred's page logic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Paper & ink makes every agreement's next action legible; refuse decorative dashboard metrics and nested card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white paper, white light rail, charcoal type and buttons, restrained green selection, thin neutral rules, system sans and the loop mark. Familiar accessible controls, deliberately precise rather than ornamental.

STORY: Identify the customer and agreement, inspect server-authoritative terms, then take a clearly labelled lifecycle action with a real result.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 250px light rail at desktop; 86px header; 42px heading at left and primary action at right; filters and a broad readable subscription table underneath. Mobile replaces the rail with a keyboard-accessible drawer. Detail shows a labelled lifecycle sequence and available next action without forcing a wizard.

FORM: User selected the top-ranked grounded candidate, Paper & ink, in the direction round seeded `b221723e`; code-led sketch `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.html`. Implement its light composition and spacing; preview data is not production content.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
