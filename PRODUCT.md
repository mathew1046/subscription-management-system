# Brightloop

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Administrators manage catalog, subscription rules and staff. Staff prepare customer quotations and operate subscriptions. Customers use the separate self-service portal; finance users inspect invoices and payments.

## Product Purpose
A self-hosted subscription-management platform for turning product and plan selections into quotations, active subscriptions and recurring invoices.

## Operating Context
Operational tables, structured editors, lifecycle actions and financial records. The current task completes Shaheem's admin/staff workflows in shaheem.md; shared presentation is redesigned while Alfred's page logic and Mathew's ongoing backend work remain untouched.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js/React and TypeScript presentation consumes the existing typed Go API. PostgreSQL and Go remain authoritative for authorization, transitions, discounts, billing and exact money. No external business services, client-side financial calculations or invented production data. Local demo records are labelled DEMO and email is captured locally by Mailpit.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the name Brightloop. The user requested a complete visual redesign that is minimal, professional, clean and responsive, with a service logo. Exact visual direction is awaiting selection.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing working backend APIs, shared contracts, role specifications and labelled local demo records. No customer testimonials or production-readiness claims are supplied.

## Product Principles
- Show real persisted state and server-returned values.
- Make configuration and lifecycle actions understandable and recoverable.
- Preserve user input on failure and prevent duplicate submission.
- Keep the operational workspace usable on desktop and mobile.
